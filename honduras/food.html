Flavors of Honduras

Honduran cuisine blends fresh ingredients and traditional recipes passed down through generations.

Baleadas

Soft flour tortillas filled with refried beans, cheese, and cream—simple and delicious.

Sopa de Caracol

Coastal classic with conch, coconut milk, and spices. Rich, comforting, and unique.

Honduran Tamales

Steamed masa with meat and vegetables, wrapped in banana leaves for a fragrant finish.
