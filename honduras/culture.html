Culture & Traditions

Honduran culture blends Indigenous, African, and Spanish roots. Festivals, music, and dance celebrate a rich and diverse heritage.

Festivals & Celebrations

From Feria Juniana in San Pedro Sula to Holy Week processions in Comayagua, traditions are celebrated with devotion and color.

Art & Music

Garifuna rhythms, drums, and folk dances express identity and community. Handicrafts and textiles showcase regional creativity.
