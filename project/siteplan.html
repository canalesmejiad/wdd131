Discover Honduras 🇭🇳

BYU-I WDD 131 — Site Plan document. This document uses the selected color scheme and typography.

Site Name

Discover Honduras — A travel & culture guide presenting destinations (Roatán, Copán Ruins), traditions (Garífuna music & dance), and traditional foods (baleadas, sopa de caracol). The name is clear, memorable, and communicates the purpose.

Optional domain: discover-honduras.org

Site Purpose

Provide an engaging, trustworthy resource to discover Honduras through three pillars:

  • Destinations: concise travel overviews, activities, and highlights for top locations (Roatán, Copán Ruins, Lake Yojoa).
  • Culture: snapshots of music, festivals, and heritage (Garífuna, Lenca, national traditions).
  • Food: introductions to iconic dishes (baleadas, sopa de caracol) with tips and brief histories.

Scenarios

  • Traveler question: “What are the best things to do in Roatán and Copán Ruins?” — The site offers activity highlights, photos, and quick tips.
  • Culture & food enthusiast: “Which traditional dishes should I try and where?” — The site introduces baleadas, sopa de caracol, and more with brief descriptions.

Typography

  • Headings: Playfair Display — elegant, readable display for H1–H3.
  • Body: Inter — highly legible sans-serif for paragraphs and UI text.
  • Logo / Emphasis: heavier Playfair Display weight for the wordmark (used sparingly).
